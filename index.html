About

I am a Data Scientist at the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP), where I build applied artificial intelligence systems for healthcare and behavioral research. My work brings together clinical natural language processing, large language models and computer vision to measure and understand human behavior and health from large-scale, real-world clinical, video and text data — with a particular focus on autism research.

Before CHOP, I held research and engineering roles at A*STAR, Hitachi Asia, Nanyang Technological University and the Indian Institute of Science, working on problems that range from intrusion detection and smart-city sensing to multilingual crisis-response NLP and theory-guided machine learning for catalysis. Across these settings, my focus has stayed the same: taking machine learning out of the benchmark and into systems that domain experts can rely on.

I hold an M.Tech in Machine Learning & Computing from the Indian Institute of Space Science & Technology, supported by a Department of Space fellowship, and a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from PES Institute of Technology, Bangalore. I have published in international journals, conferences and edited volumes, received multiple best paper awards, and serve as a peer reviewer in applied AI, computer vision and machine learning.
